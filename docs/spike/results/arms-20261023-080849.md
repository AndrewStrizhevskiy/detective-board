## Interpretation and caveats

- Best by (all hit@5, MRR): `hyb[or] k=30 w=0.5:1`: hit@1 0.63, hit@5 0.97, hit@10 0.97, MRR 0.75, recall@10 0.96. Vector only: 0.57 / 0.89 / 0.94 / 0.70 / 0.91. Gain: +2 questions at hit@1 (22 vs 20), +3 at hit@5 (34 vs 31), +1 at hit@10. BM25 alone: hit@5 0.69.
- Weights 0.5:1 and 1:2 are the same ranking (RRF is scale-invariant), so they tie everywhere; the real finding is "BM25 weight below the vector weight". With 1:1, k=10/30 give hit@5 0.91 and k=60 slightly worse on cross. k in {30, 60} are indistinguishable at 0.5:1.
- BM25 AND mode is useless for natural-language questions (0 hits: no chunk contains all question words; only 1 of 35 returns anything after stop-word removal). OR mode is the only usable BM25 mode.
- Stop-word removal for BM25 does not help: slightly worse alone (hit@5 0.66 vs 0.69) and in hybrid (hit@1 0.57 vs 0.63). FTS5 bm25 already down-weights frequent words by IDF, so the trick is redundant. For Russian nothing was done (nothing to do: no Cyrillic in the English corpus).
- Russian (n=5): BM25 returns an empty list for 5/5 questions, hence 0.00 everywhere; hybrid equals vector-only exactly (hit@5 1.00, MRR 0.71) because the empty BM25 arm adds no RRF terms and does not hurt. Russian retrieval is entirely bge-m3's cross-lingual vector arm.
- Failures of the best config at k=5: one question, hard-04 (rank 65). The question describes the stage background ("actress", "male costume") in paraphrase; gold is the Adler letter chunk whose wording ("trained as an actress", "male costume") only partly overlaps; vector rank is outside top 100, BM25 rank 10, and RRF with w_bm25=0.5 pushes a BM25-only candidate below all vector top-100 candidates. Single gold chunk, not a boundary issue; it is a wording/paraphrase miss. Vector-only also misses en-09 (rank 14), en-12 (8), hard-02 (6), all rescued by BM25 lexical overlap.
- Reranker signal: at k=5 only 1 miss remains for the best hybrid and it is not recoverable by reranking the top 10 (rank 65). At k=1, 13 of 35 miss and 12 of those 13 have the first gold chunk at rank 2-10 (vector-only: 13 of 15 at rank 2-10), so a reranker has headroom for precision@1 / MRR but little for hit@5.
- Caveats: n=35 (5 Russian, 3 cross), so one question is 2.9 points; differences of 1-3 questions are not statistically supported, there is no CI, and hyperparameters (k, weights) were selected on the same 35 questions that they are scored on (optimistic). Gold = any chunk touching an evidence quote, which can understate hits when another chunk also answers; the question set was partly built by the same project, with possible lexical leakage into BM25. Depth is 100 per arm, MRR beyond 100 counts 0. Ties in cosine are broken by corpus order; Ollama bge-m3 query vectors use default num_ctx.
