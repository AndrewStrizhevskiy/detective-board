## Caveats (read before quoting)

- NOT a clean window: the laptop also ran the university VPN client, a second VPN (sing-box), Docker, the desktop and the
  assistant app. Load average before the run: 0.2. The numbers are an upper bound for the speed, not a best case.
- One run, no repeats, no 8 GB memory profile, no 3-run median: the procedure of issue 14 is NOT complete. Do not present these as final.
- The temperature climbed from 79 to 92 C over six consecutive questions: thermal throttling is likely in a long demo (charger, cooling pad, short breaks).
- g009 (a multi-hop question about the chain of people) was REFUSED although it is answerable in the gold set: a known limit of single-sentence extractive answers.
- Latency is dominated by the prefill of the selection prompt (about 21 tokens/s on this CPU), see `ttft-levers-2026-10-26.md`.
